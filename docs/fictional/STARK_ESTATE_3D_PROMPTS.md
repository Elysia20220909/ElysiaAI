# Cliffside AI Lab Residence 3D Prompt Pack

Purpose: generate a Stark-estate-like original cliffside residence for concept art,
modeling blockouts, texture references, and cinematic previs. Keep it as an
original "cliffside AI lab residence", not a one-to-one replica of the MCU house.

## Core Visual DNA

- Cliff-top concrete and glass residence overlooking the Pacific.
- Organic modernist massing inspired by late 20th-century concrete architecture.
- Low horizontal slabs, terraced forms, curved roof geometry, ocean-facing glass.
- Strong cantilever over a rocky cliff, with exposed structural confidence.
- Upper level feels like a gallery-like social house.
- Lower level feels like the real core: workshop, garage, robotics lab, armor-scale storage.
- House reads as a livable research reactor: luxury shell, industrial heart.

## Camera Recipes

| Shot | Lens | Camera Position | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Establishing exterior | 28-35mm | Ocean side, slightly below slab | Make cantilever feel dangerous but plausible |

## Material Palette

| Material | Look |
| --- | --- |
| Exterior concrete | Warm white, fine aggregate, subtle formwork seams |
| Interior floor | Polished concrete or dark basalt tile, low gloss |
| Glass | Slight blue-green edge tint, high transparency, strong reflections |
| Frames | Satin black steel or gunmetal aluminum |
| Workshop floor | Sealed industrial concrete with tire marks and tool wear |
| Lighting | Warm residential lines upstairs, cooler task lights downstairs |
| Cliff | Layered tan-gray coastal rock, erosion detail, sparse vegetation |

## Scale Anchors

- Main living span: 18-28 m wide.
- Ocean-facing glass height: 3.2-4.5 m.
- Cantilever expression: 5-9 m visible projection, with hidden transfer structure.
- Workshop floor-to-ceiling: 5-7 m for lifts, arms, and tall storage.
- Vehicle ramp width: 4-5 m minimum.
- Elevator core: connects entry, living, private level, and workshop.
