# Avengers Compound Image Prompt Pack

Purpose: Avengers Compound / New Avengers Facility 風の建築、内観、格納庫、
量子ラボ、崩壊後バリアントを画像生成し、3D制作や美術設計の参考にする。
必要に応じて「original hero operations campus」として出力し、既存作品の
完全コピーを避ける。

## Core Visual DNA

- Upstate New York style private campus surrounded by lawn, trees, water, and service roads.
- Low horizontal modern buildings with white metal panels and wide glass curtain walls.
- Museum-like calm on the outside; military airport and advanced lab logic underneath.
- Large hangar volume, VTOL apron, vehicle bays, hidden sublevel infrastructure.
- Clean corporate research architecture with subtle superhero-scale security.
- Tone: bright, quiet, expensive, slightly lonely, ready for global crisis.

## Prompt Use Notes

- For exact MCU reference studies, keep source labels in the art board.
- For reusable production assets, prefer original signage and an abstract team emblem.
- Keep the architecture grounded: if the hangar is huge, add service roads, drainage,
  roof equipment, maintenance doors, and scale references.
- Avoid turning the compound into a luxury mansion. It is a team base first.
